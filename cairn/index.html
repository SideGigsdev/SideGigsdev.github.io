The obvious question

Why not just use ChatGPT for this?

You can. I did, for months, and it kind of works — that's actually why I started building this. But there are three things a chat window can't do, and they turn out to be most of the job.

01

It doesn't forget

Not a summary of what you told it. An actual record — every day you logged, every deadline, every time you changed your mind about something. Still there in six months, still searchable.

02

It turns up on its own

The brief is there at 07:40 whether or not you opened anything. A chat window only ever answers you. Turning up without being asked is most of what a secretary actually does.

03

It remembers what you promised

It knows what you said you'd change last Sunday and whether you did. Not to tell you off — to work out which bit of the plan was wrong, so you can fix that instead of trying harder.

On purpose

Things I've decided it won't do

A lot of apps here run on tricks that get you opening them more and feeling worse. I've written these into the repo so you can hold me to them.

  • It won't reset your streak. Missing one day doesn't actually set you back — that's in the research. Every app resets you anyway, because losing a number makes you open the app. You get one repair a week instead.
  • No random rewards. Unpredictable rewards are what slot machines run on. It would work. I'm not doing it.
  • No fake urgency, no guilt notifications, and nothing that pings you about something you've already done.
  • No leaderboards, no sharing your streak. Your Thursdays aren't failing because of other people.
  • You can leave whenever. Everything exports to plain JSON and imports straight back, and the same file works in the Mac app.
